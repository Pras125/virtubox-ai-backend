# Print Kiosk (by VirtuBox)

Transcribed from the Drive one-pagers "Print Kiosk (Features)" and "Print Kiosk (Specifications)".

## Positioning
Scan. Pay. Print. In minutes. Fast, reliable document printing experience.
Long queues and manual handling slow down document services. The Print Kiosk is a smart, self-service solution that lets customers print, scan and copy documents independently through a simple touchscreen interface. It delivers instant, high-quality document printing in high-traffic environments, reduces dependency on manual counters, and ensures quick, seamless service. Fast service, less staff dependency, better customer experience. Documents are fetched with a pick-up code or QR code and delivered in seconds.

## Benefits ("The kiosk that prints profit")
- Print anytime: customers print documents independently without staff.
- Faster service: reduce queues and handle more users during peak hours.
- Increase revenue: offer printing, scanning, copying and add-on services.
- Premium setup: enhances your store image and attracts more customers.
- Lower operating costs: reduce staff workload and improve profitability.
- Scalable solution: add more kiosks easily across multiple locations.
